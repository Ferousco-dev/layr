## Review notes

- HTTP success/failure schemas, status codes, request-ID behaviour, CLI verbs, exit semantics, JSON Lines logs, lifecycle events, and stable errors are baselined.
- No response or error may expose a stack trace, SQL, DSN, internal hostname, credential, raw request data, or secret value.
- M1.1 has no product GUI and this gate does not authorize identity, OAuth, Figma, project, import, generation, or export interfaces.
- Runtime usability claims remain unverified until construction and verification. The usability record is explicitly a heuristic specification review, not a user study.
