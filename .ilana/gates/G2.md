## Architecture checklist evidence

- Decomposition: single-process modular monolith with one composition root and acyclic packages.
- State: PostgreSQL is sole durable authority; Redis is empty/ephemeral at M1.1; migration metadata is tool-owned.
- Interfaces: inputs, outputs, errors, preconditions, and postconditions are listed; detailed specs define invariants, algorithms, errors, concurrency, and test hooks.
- Security/operations: five trust boundaries, allow-listed logging, bounded contexts, deterministic shutdown, truthful liveness/readiness, and licence caveats are explicit.
- Scope: no user, OAuth, project, import, asset, Design IR, AI, Chromium, export, or object-store design was introduced.
